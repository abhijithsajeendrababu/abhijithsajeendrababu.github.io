Python Mini Cup

A 2D penalty shootout game built with Python and Pygame.

About the Game

I built Python Mini Cup for my first-semester Programming module. The goal is to score as many penalties as you can against a goalkeeper who moves from side to side. Every goal adds to your score and makes the goalkeeper faster.

How to Play

  • Use the mouse to aim and click to shoot.
  • A saved or missed shot ends the game.
  • After game over, click or press R to try again.
